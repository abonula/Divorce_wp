"A dignified path through one of life's hardest transitions — built around your family, not the courts."

Collaborative divorce is a structured, voluntary process in which both spouses work with a team of trained professionals to reach fair agreements — no courtroom required. The result is faster, less costly, and far less damaging to the families involved.

CDGW brings together certified collaborative professionals across Southeast Michigan to offer a seamless, compassionate experience from start to finish.

The Process

How collaborative divorce works

Four structured stages that keep you in control from start to finish.

1

Initial Consultation

Each spouse meets privately with a collaborative attorney to understand the process, their rights, and whether this path fits their situation.

2

Build Your Team

You assemble a neutral team: two collaborative attorneys, a financial neutral, and a coach or child specialist as needed.

3

Structured Sessions

The full team meets in facilitated sessions to address all issues — property, finances, parenting — at a pace that works for your family.

4

Final Agreement

Once all decisions are made, attorneys draft a binding settlement. You both sign, and the court approves it — without a hearing.

Your Team

Who's on your collaborative team

Three disciplines working together as one coordinated unit — so nothing falls through the cracks.

Attorneys

Each spouse has their own collaborative attorney — trained to facilitate agreement, not escalate conflict. They advise, negotiate, and draft all legal documents with resolution as the goal.

Financial Specialists

A neutral financial professional helps both parties understand their complete financial picture, model settlement options, and make informed decisions about property, support, and long-term planning.

Mental Health Professionals

Divorce coaches and child specialists help manage emotions, improve communication, and ensure the needs of children are at the center of every agreement. They help families process the transition constructively.
