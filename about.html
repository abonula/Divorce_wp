Who We Are

About CDGW

A professional group dedicated to advancing collaborative divorce practice throughout Southeast Michigan.

Our Mission

Why we exist

Collaborative Divorce of Greater Washtenaw was founded to make collaborative divorce more accessible to families throughout Washtenaw County and its surrounding communities — including Livingston, Jackson, and beyond.

"We believe families deserve a dignified, private path through divorce — one that preserves relationships, protects children, and respects everyone involved."

Our group brings together attorneys, financial professionals, and mental health specialists who share a commitment to the collaborative process. By working together as an organized community of practice, we improve the quality of collaborative services available across the region and make it easier for clients to assemble the right team.

The name "Greater Washtenaw" reflects our reach beyond Washtenaw County proper — honoring the surrounding communities we serve without listing each by name.

What Guides Us

Our values

Peaceful Resolution

We believe conflict can be resolved without combat. Every member of our group is trained to facilitate agreement, not escalation.

Client-Centered

The needs of the families we serve — not professional egos or billing incentives — guide every decision in the collaborative process.

Transparency

Full, honest disclosure of financial and personal information is a cornerstone of collaborative practice. We hold ourselves and our clients to this standard.

Interdisciplinary

Legal, financial, and emotional dimensions of divorce are inseparable. Our team-based approach addresses all of them in one integrated process.

Children First

When children are involved, their wellbeing shapes every agreement. We include specialists trained to support children through family transitions.

Professionalism

Our members maintain training in collaborative practice, uphold ethical standards, and participate actively in our professional community.

Our Region

Serving Southeast Michigan

Our members practice primarily in and around Washtenaw County — home to Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti, Saline, Chelsea, and many surrounding communities. We also serve clients in Livingston County (Brighton, Howell), Jackson County, Ingham County, and neighboring areas.

The collaborative process does not require court appearances in most cases, which means geographic flexibility for our clients. If you're unsure whether a member serves your area, we encourage you to reach out directly.
